# 90–180 Day Investor & Production Roadmap

Ref: `handoff/CP_FINAL_TASKS_V2.md` §E.

**Horizon:** 90 to 180 days after the hackathon submission window closes.
**Goal:** convert the primitives + 30-day SDKs into a production-ready,
investor-legible business with a first paying design partner in
production, a documented security posture, and a data room a serious
seed investor can read in a day.

## Positioning

- **Buyer profile:** regulated AI/DeFi infrastructure teams that need
  cryptographic accountability for agent decisions — risk desks at
  quantitative funds, compliance vendors, tokenised-asset issuers.
- **Not the buyer:** general-purpose "we want blockchain" prospects.
- **Value proposition:** a proof primitive that binds an agent decision
  to (a) a model version, (b) a signed evidence root, (c) an on-chain
  commitment with objective slashing on equivocation.
- **Unit of value:** a verified decision. Everything is priced against
  that unit.

## Pricing model (draft)

- **Design partner tier:** flat monthly fee + ≤ N verifications/mo, price
  set by design-partner-specific terms; goal is signal, not revenue.
- **Metered tier:** per-verification pricing with volume tiers; gross
  margin target ≥ 70% at steady-state.
- **Enterprise tier:** flat annual + SLA + private support Slack; priced
  against buyer's cost-of-regulatory-audit.

Design partner sign-up is the primary KPI for the 90-day slice; ARR is
the primary KPI for the 180-day slice.

## Objectives

1. Multi-tenant + RBAC + billing (see `docs/roadmap/MULTITENANCY.md`).
2. Prod SLOs + incident response (see `docs/roadmap/SLO.md`).
3. Legal / compliance / retention (see `docs/roadmap/LEGAL.md`).
4. Production key management + evidence retention (see
   `docs/roadmap/KEY_MANAGEMENT.md`).
5. Investor data room ready (see `docs/roadmap/DATA_ROOM.md`).

## Deliverables timeline

- **Days 0–30 (post-hackathon-day-30):**
  - Multi-tenant model live in staging.
  - Prod SLO / incident policy in `docs/roadmap/SLO.md` signed off.
  - First design partner MSA drafted.
- **Days 30–90:**
  - First paying design partner in production.
  - Third-party cryptography audit RFP out; ≥ 3 vendor bids in.
  - Trusted-setup ceremony executed (see `docs/roadmap/CEREMONY.md`).
- **Days 90–180:**
  - Third-party audit report published under `docs/audits/`.
  - ≥ 2 paying design partners; first case study.
  - Investor data room complete; first partner meetings.

## Acceptance criteria (day 180)

- [ ] `docs/roadmap/MULTITENANCY.md`, `SLO.md`, `LEGAL.md`,
      `KEY_MANAGEMENT.md`, `DATA_ROOM.md` all present.
- [ ] `docs/audits/<vendor>-<date>.pdf` present (report from the third-party
      cryptography audit).
- [ ] ≥ 2 paying design partners live.
- [ ] Status page live with rolling 90-day uptime.
- [ ] Ceremony transcript published under `crypto/ceremony/transcripts/`.

## Non-goals for the 180-day window

- Mainnet Casper deployment. Deferred until the Condor 2.x pairing
  precompile ships (or a documented workaround is agreed with the
  Casper team).
- Public token / airdrop. Distraction; not aligned with regulated-AI
  buyer profile.
- Full recursive-ZK aggregation. Documented in
  `docs/roadmap/RECURSIVE_ZK.md` (roadmap, not shipped).
